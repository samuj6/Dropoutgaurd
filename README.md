# DropoutGuard – AI Early Warning & Intervention System

> **Identify Early. Intervene Early. Prevent Dropout.**

DropoutGuard is an AI-based early warning and intervention system designed to identify students who may be at risk of dropping out **2–3 months in advance**.

The system uses data already available in a school's Management Information System (MIS), such as attendance, academic performance, absence patterns, and relevant local/context factors.

Instead of reacting to a single attendance drop, DropoutGuard analyzes **patterns and trends** and provides an explainable **“WHY + WHAT NEXT?”** intervention layer.

---

## Problem Statement

Government schools may identify a student as having effectively dropped out only after months of unexplained absence. By then, timely intervention can become difficult.

A temporary attendance drop may be caused by a family emergency, illness, or another short-term situation. Therefore, the system considers trends and context instead of treating every attendance drop as a dropout risk.

**DropoutGuard aims to identify genuine dropout risk early and support timely intervention.**

---

## Proposed Solution

DropoutGuard follows this workflow:

School MIS Data
      ↓
Attendance + Marks + Absence Patterns + Local Factors
      ↓
Data Processing & Trend Analysis
      ↓
Machine Learning Model
      ↓
Dropout Risk Score
      ↓
WHY? — Explain the Risk
      ↓
WHAT NEXT? — Recommend an Action
      ↓
Teacher Dashboard
      ↓
Intervention Tracking
